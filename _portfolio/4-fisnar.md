FisnarRobotPlugin is a plugin for Ultimaker's Cura slicing software that runs Fisnar gantry robots and pneumatic dispensers for high-precision 3D printing, manual control, and pick-and-place. It is published on the <a href="https://marketplace.ultimaker.com/app/cura/plugins/Spenbert02/FisnarRobotPlugin" target="_blank">Ultimaker Marketplace</a>, with over 14,000 downloads. Below: a silicone heart valve printed with a hydrogel support (top) and a multi-material University of Minnesota logo (bottom).
